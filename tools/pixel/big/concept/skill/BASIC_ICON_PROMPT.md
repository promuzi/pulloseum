Style for all 3 (simple on purpose):
- One big, bold, iconic subject filling about 70 percent of the square, centered; nothing small or fiddly.
- Flat cel shading with only 3 to 4 tones per color, a thick dark outline around the main shapes, no painterly texture, no fine detail, no tiny particles.
- Strong contrast between the subject and the background. Background = a plain soft gradient in muted steel blue-grey (these moves have no element), slightly darker toward the edges.
- Fresh leaf greens for the leaves, a warm pale yellow-white for impact flashes.
- Charming game-icon look, like simple ability icons; no character face is needed (the leaf is the hero).
- Square 1254x1254, no text, no letters, no numbers, no frame, no border, no watermark.
